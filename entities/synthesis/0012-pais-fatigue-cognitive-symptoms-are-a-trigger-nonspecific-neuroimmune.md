This is suggestive support for hypothesis:0012-pais-fatigue-cognitive-symptoms-are-a-trigger-nonspecific-neuroimmune's trigger-nonspecific framing, though interpretation:0039-t108-cross-pais-il6-peak-vs-pais-risk is desk-research grade and explicitly mints no proposition-level belief updates.
