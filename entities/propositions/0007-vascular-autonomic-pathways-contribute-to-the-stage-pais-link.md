# Proposition: Vascular and autonomic pathways contribute to the reproductive-stage-PAIS link independent of adaptive autoimmunity

## Claim

Vascular and autonomic pathways **contribute to** the reproductive-stage–PAIS association partly **independent of classical adaptive autoimmunity** — i.e. an endothelial / thromboinflammatory / dysautonomic channel sits alongside any antibody-mediated one.

## Evidence Summary

`paper:Kawai2025` links viral infection to vascular and thromboinflammatory outcomes that could interact with menopause-associated cardiometabolic and endothelial risk, supplying analogical plausibility for a vascular/autonomic contribution.

## Caveats

Indirect and analogical. "Contributes to" is stated as a **mechanism-contribution claim, not a formal mediation edge**: per the epistemic-edges facet, mediation roles ("X mediates the effect of stage on PAIS") are higher-order / derived from patch topology and are deliberately not authored here. `background` role; overlaps the immune-pathway mechanism `proposition:0002` but names a distinct (non-adaptive-autoimmune) channel.
