# Proposition: ME/CFS has exercise-provoked skeletal-muscle bioenergetic abnormalities

## Claim

ME/CFS/CFS has objective skeletal-muscle bioenergetic abnormalities under exercise or contraction-like stress, including abnormal intramuscular acidosis/recovery on 31P-MRS and impaired contraction-stimulated metabolic signaling in patient-derived skeletal-muscle cells. This supports the **muscle-localization side** of `hypothesis:0006` and partially fills the previously empty ME/CFS muscle-endpoint cell in `discussion:0004`.

The claim is deliberately narrower than h0006-A1. It does **not** yet show that ME/CFS and long COVID share the **same Appelman2024-type post-PEM muscle lesion** under a harmonized protocol. The existing ME/CFS endpoints are heterogeneous: 31P-MRS pH/phosphocreatine/ATP dynamics, in-vitro AMPK/glucose-uptake response, and resting biopsy OXPHOS/morphology. They are muscle-local and bioenergetic, but not equivalent to Appelman2024's pre/post-exertion OXPHOS capacity, SDH activity, fiber-type shift, immune infiltrate, and exercise-induced myopathy.

## Evidence Summary

- **Jones2012:** CDC-1994 CFS patients and sedentary controls underwent repeated exercise with 31P-MRS. The subgroup achieving normal phosphocreatine depletion developed greater acidosis after comparable work and had nearly four-fold prolonged pH recovery. This is the strongest t056 line because it is in-vivo, exercise-provoked, and muscle-specific.
- **Wong1992:** 31P-NMR during graded gastrocnemius exercise found CFS patients reached exhaustion faster, with similar qualitative pH/phosphocreatine patterns but significantly lower ATP at exhaustion; interpreted as impaired oxidative metabolism with accelerated glycolysis.
- **Brown2015:** CFS-derived skeletal-muscle cultures failed to increase AMPK phosphorylation and glucose uptake after electrical pulse stimulation, despite intact insulin-stimulated glucose uptake. This supports a contraction-response defect retained in muscle cells.
- **Bizjak2024:** resting muscle-biopsy respirometry/electron microscopy showed muscle mitochondrial functional/morphological abnormalities in CFS and post-COVID syndrome, but with trigger-different specific phenotypes. This strengthens muscle-localization while warning against a simple same-lesion conclusion.

## Caveats

This proposition is **not** a promotion criterion discharge for `hypothesis:0006`. It advances the interim A1 probe from "no ME/CFS muscle-endpoint datum" to "ME/CFS muscle-endpoint abnormalities exist, but are not harmonized with long-COVID post-PEM biopsy." The decisive h0006-A1 test remains a same-protocol ME/CFS and long-COVID provocation design with pre/immediate/24-48 h post-exercise muscle biopsy (OXPHOS, SDH/Complex II, fiber-type, ionic markers, myopathic injury) and matched controls.

The older 31P-MRS studies use broad CFS criteria and do not phenotype PEM with modern instruments. Brown2015 is an in-vitro contraction model. Bizjak2024 is direct muscle biopsy but resting-state, not exercise-provoked. These limitations are exactly why this claim is scoped to a local proposition and does not directly grade h0006's full ischemic-ionic cascade.
