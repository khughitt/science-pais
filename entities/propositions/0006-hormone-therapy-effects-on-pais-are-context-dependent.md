# Proposition: Hormone-therapy effects on PAIS, if present, are route/dose/timing/indication-dependent

## Claim

Hormone-therapy effects on PAIS, **if present, are route-, dose-, timing-, indication-, and comorbidity-dependent** rather than uniformly protective or harmful. The claim is about the *heterogeneity* of any HRT effect, not its average sign.

## Evidence Summary

Direct HRT → PAIS-outcome evidence is sparse in this corpus. `paper:Averyanova2022` supplies only indirect biological plausibility for route- and timing-dependent hormone effects on immune, endothelial, and hemostatic systems. As cited, this proposition is currently **more prediction than established result**.

## Caveats

Thinly evidenced — a fragility signal flagged for `task:t019` (HRT-evidence audit). Observational HRT–PAIS associations are dominated by healthy-user and indication biases, so an apparent uniform protective or harmful effect is the expected artifact this proposition warns against. `background` role: it refines but does not constitute `hypothesis:0005`.
