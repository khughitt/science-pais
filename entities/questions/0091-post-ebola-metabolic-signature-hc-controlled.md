# Does the post-Ebola metabolomic signature extend to fatigue-phenotype PES and replicate against healthy uninfected controls?

## Summary

`paper:Sanford2026` (medRxiv preprint) is the only source grading the post-Ebola leg of the cross-pathogen PAIS matrix (`search:0002`). Its primary finding — a TCA/amino-acid/SCFA metabolic signature distinguishing EVD survivors with PES from asymptomatic survivors — carries two compounding limitations that make it insufficient to confirm or deny a shared metabolic attractor with LC/ME/CFS: (1) the comparison to household controls (healthy uninfected individuals) did NOT reach statistical significance, so the signature may be a generic EVD-survivor state rather than PAIS-specific; (2) the PES case definition covers MSK/GI and cardiopulmonary sequelae, not the fatigue/cognitive/PEM phenotype central to `hypothesis:0001`. This question asks whether a future study, using healthy-control comparisons and a fatigue-phenotype case definition, would replicate a signature comparable to LC/ME/CFS metabolomic profiles.

## Why It Matters

- **Affects the post-Ebola cell of the cross-trigger matrix:** `hypothesis:0001`'s cross-pathogen breadth relies on this cell. If the HC-controlled, fatigue-phenotype replication fails, the post-Ebola leg must be downgraded from "thin-but-present" to "absent for the fatigue attractor."
- **Methodological precedent for comparator design:** If the survivor-internal comparison is systematically different from the HC-controlled comparison (as the null HC result in Sanford2026 suggests), cross-trigger inference from survivor-internal studies will be systematically biased — affecting how all similar post-EVD and post-outbreak studies are read.
- **Biomarker implications:** The 10-metabolite panel with AUC > 0.8 (internal validation only) has no clear biomarker utility unless it replicates against uninfected controls.

## Current Evidence

- **Sanford2026 (preprint):** PES vs asymptomatic survivors — 34 differentially expressed metabolites (FDR < 0.05, FC > 2.0); TCA, amino-acid, nucleotide, SCFA pathways downregulated. BUT: PES vs HCs — no significant differential metabolites.
- **The HC-null** is a critical negative that cannot be dismissed as merely under-powered — it changes the interpretation of the positive finding from "PAIS-specific" to "possibly survivor-internal amplification."
- **Phenotype gap:** No post-Ebola study has profiled fatigue/PEM-phenotype PES metabolomics; this is a true data vacuum.
- **Cross-trigger context:** LC and ME/CFS metabolomic signatures are all compared against healthy controls (Shankar2025, Walitt2024, Che2025) — making the Sanford2026 comparator non-comparable by design.

## Thoughts

- **Best current interpretation:** The Sanford2026 finding is a real survivor-internal metabolic signal of unknown specificity. Whether it reflects failed immune homeostasis (PAIS-like) or a persistent infection-induced metabolic shift in all survivors cannot be determined from the current data.
- **Major uncertainty:** Whether EVD survivors who develop *fatigue* phenotype PES share the same metabolic signature as those with MSK/GI and CP sequelae is entirely unknown.
- **Operational priority:** Low in the near term (no dataset or collaboration available); important to flag as a structural gap when citing Sanford2026 in support of `hypothesis:0001`.
