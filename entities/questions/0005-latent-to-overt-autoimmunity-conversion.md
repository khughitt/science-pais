# What fraction of latent post-infectious autoimmunity converts to overt autoimmune disease over 5-10 years, and which autoantibodies mark the highest-risk subset?

## Summary

Post-infectious autoimmunity is broad and common — latent autoimmunity is near-universal in symptomatic PCS (Rojas2022), and new-onset autoimmune disease incidence rises after SARS-CoV-2 in very large cohorts (Sharma2023). But most of this autoimmunity is *latent* (autoantibodies without clinical disease). This question asks what fraction converts to overt autoimmune disease over a 5-10 year horizon and which autoantibody subsets carry the highest risk — distinguishing durable immune reprogramming from a resolving, transient mimicry response.

## Why It Matters

- Decides whether post-infectious latent autoimmunity warrants long-term clinical monitoring and possible early intervention, and whether PAIS represents a permanent shift in immune set-point or a recoverable perturbation.
- If unanswered, the prognostic meaning of post-COVID autoantibody findings is unknown, risking both over-treatment (chasing latent markers) and under-surveillance (missing converters).

## Current Evidence

- Supporting: Sharma2023 documents elevated new-onset RA/SLE/T1DM/IBD/vasculitis hazard ratios post-SARS-CoV-2; Rojas2022 finds latent autoimmunity in 83% and polyautoimmunity in 62% at ~7 months, with anti-IFN-λ linked to respiratory-symptom persistence; Ciaffi2023 reports persistently elevated ACPA at 8 months in convalescents.
- Conflicting / cautionary: only ~3% of Rojas2022 patients had overt disease at 7 months; all cohorts have ≤15-month follow-up (Sharma2023), so conversion over years is unmeasured; temporal association does not establish causation, and mimicry responses may resolve.

## Thoughts

- Best current interpretation: latent autoimmunity is a real, frequent post-infectious state whose long-term clinical weight is genuinely unknown; anti-cytokine/anti-IFN autoantibodies are the most plausible high-risk subset to track (Rojas2022).
- Major uncertainty: the latency between latent and overt autoimmunity can be years, so existing follow-up windows cannot answer the conversion rate; pre-existing autoimmunity (reduced regulatory reserve, Sharma2023) confounds risk attribution.
