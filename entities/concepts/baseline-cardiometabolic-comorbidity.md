# Baseline cardiometabolic comorbidity

Pre-infection (and largely pre-/peri-menopause) cardiometabolic disease. **Confounder** of the total effect once the `baseline_comorbidity -> menopause-timing` edge is drawn (metabolic disease/smoking accelerate menopause). v2 split of the old single comorbidity node; carried as a **sensitivity** covariate, not primary (t029 Q2 default).

Variable in the t014/t023 menopause-PAIS causal DAG v2 (`patch-definition:menopause-pais-causal-dag`).
