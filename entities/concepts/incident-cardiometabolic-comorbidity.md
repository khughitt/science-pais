# Incident (menopause-driven) cardiometabolic comorbidity

Post-/peri-menopause incident cardiometabolic disease arising downstream of the hormone shift (menopause -> sex hormones -> incident comorbidity -> PAIS). **Mediator** of the total effect — do NOT adjust. v2 split partner of baseline comorbidity; the split is what makes the `baseline -> menopause` edge acyclic.

Variable in the t014/t023 menopause-PAIS causal DAG v2 (`patch-definition:menopause-pais-causal-dag`).
