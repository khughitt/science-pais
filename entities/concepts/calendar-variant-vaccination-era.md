# Calendar period / variant / vaccination era

Era of acute infection (calendar period, SARS-CoV-2 variant, vaccination availability). Common cause of acute severity and of who is studied. **Confounder of mediator -> outcome paths** (relevant to the severity-controlled direct effect) and a selection node; not a cause of menopause timing, so off the total-effect back-door unless linked via shielding behaviour (noted structural alternative, not drawn).
