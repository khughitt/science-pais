# Pre-existing atopy / mast-cell activation hyperreactivity

The intended construct is a **pre-infection** primed-mast-cell / elevated-IgE state (diagnosed MCAS, or atopy
as a proxy) present *before* the triggering infection — the mechanistic inversion of the project's usual
treatment of mast-cell activation as a post-infectious consequence.

**Construct-validity caveat (load-bearing).** The available evidence does not cleanly measure this construct:
- **`cite:Wolff2023`** concerns **atopic disease (asthma, rhinitis, eczema)**, and its own evidence grade is
  *very uncertain* — **atopy is not diagnosed MCAS**.
- The **Augustin** gut-immune-axis work (now a full paper: Mucosal Immunology 2026,
  doi:10.1016/j.mucimm.2026.03.002, PMID 41794369; cross-sectional, N=43, 15–22 months *post*-infection)
  measures mast-cell activity **after** infection with **no pre-infection baseline** — it is
  **consequence-side** evidence and **cannot ground a pre-infection effect-modifier arrow**.

So in the compound-boundary DAG (`patch-definition:compound-boundary-conditions-interaction-dag`,
question:0034) this node is a **hypothesized** pre-infection modifier whose exposure definition (diagnosed
MCAS vs atopy proxy) and pre-infection ascertainment are **unmet**. Its conjectured amplification of
`concept:th2-mast-cell-axis` and phenotype-shift toward histamine/prostaglandin-dominated presentations, and
its pairing with `concept:pregnancy-state-immune-milieu` (structurally sign-ambiguous), are untested.
