# Pre-existing atopy / mast-cell activation hyperreactivity

Constitutively primed mast cells and elevated IgE *before* the triggering infection (atopy, diagnosed MCAS) — the mechanistic *inversion* of the project's usual treatment of mast-cell activation as a post-infectious consequence: here mast cells are the upstream amplifier (Wolff2023 allergic-disease → long-COVID association; Augustin2025 n=21 ileal mast-cell/tryptase/spike signal).

A **host-modifier** in the compound-boundary DAG (`patch-definition:compound-boundary-conditions-interaction-dag`, question:0034). It amplifies the **Th2/mast-cell axis** (`concept:th2-mast-cell-axis`) and shifts the PAIS *phenotype* toward histamine/prostaglandin-dominated presentations. Paired with `concept:pregnancy-state-immune-milieu` it forms the DAG's distinct-route focal pair, where the two modifiers' edges into the shared Th2/mast-cell node can be same- or opposite-signed (pregnancy Th2 shift could prime *or*, via Treg tolerance, dampen mast-cell triggering) — a structurally sign-ambiguous interaction.
