# Th2 / mast-cell effector axis

The Th2-polarized, mast-cell/histamine-driven effector route to PAIS — a **phenotype-specific mediator** carrying histamine/prostaglandin/leukotriene-dominated presentations (urticaria, flushing, GI symptoms, hyperadrenergic POTS) distinct from the Th1/autoimmune-neuroinflammatory and thrombovascular routes.

The convergence node for the DAG's **distinct-route focal pair** (`patch-definition:compound-boundary-conditions-interaction-dag`, question:0057): `concept:mast-cell-activation-hyperreactivity` amplifies it, and `concept:pregnancy-state-immune-milieu`'s Th2 shift also feeds it — but pregnancy's simultaneous Treg/tolerance arm can *dampen* mast-cell triggering, so the two modifiers' net effect on this node is structurally sign-ambiguous. Because this axis colors PAIS *phenotype* rather than only its incidence, it is the node that makes the pregnancy × MCAS interaction a **phenotype-redirection** question (`hypothesis:0020` P5) that a single incidence outcome cannot capture.
