# Unmeasured shared confounders

Latent common causes of reproductive stage and PAIS outside the measured set: socioeconomic status, prior EBV/autoimmunity, genetic/HLA risk, health behaviours. An open back-door threatening identifiability.

Variable in the t014 causal DAG (`patch-definition:menopause-pais-causal-dag`).
