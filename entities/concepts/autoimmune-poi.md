# Autoimmune premature ovarian insufficiency

Autoimmune POI / autoimmune common cause: autoimmunity -> early menopause (autoimmune oophoritis/thyroid) AND -> PAIS predisposition. A **distinct etiologic-stratum confounder**, not a generic autoimmune-dx (20002) adjustment. Drawn explicitly in v2; handled as a stratum/quarantine + sensitivity, not primary (t029 Q3).
