# Pregnancy-state immune milieu (active gestation/peripartum)

The immune remodeling of *active* pregnancy — expanded Tregs, Th2/IL-4/IL-10 polarization, suppressed Th1/IFN-γ, complement and coagulation shifts — with rapid postpartum reconstitution. **Distinct from `concept:pregnancy-history`/parity** (a life-course staging input); this node is the transient gestational *state* at time of infection.

A **host-modifier** in the compound-boundary DAG (`patch-definition:compound-boundary-conditions-interaction-dag`, question:0040). Its edges are **opposite-signed by route**: it *dampens* the Th1/autoimmune/neuroinflammatory route (via Treg/Th2 shift → `concept:immune-dysregulation`; Bruno2024 lower cognitive/fatigue PASC) while *preserving or amplifying* the thrombovascular route (`concept:thromboinflammation-and-endothelial-dysfunction`; Bruno2024 higher cardiac/thromboembolic PASC) and feeding the **Th2/mast-cell axis** (`concept:th2-mast-cell-axis`). With `concept:mast-cell-activation-hyperreactivity` it forms the DAG's **distinct/opposite-signed-route** focal pair — the case where compound exposure redirects *phenotype*, not just incidence.
