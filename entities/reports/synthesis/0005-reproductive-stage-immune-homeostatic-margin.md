## State

hypothesis:0005-reproductive-stage-immune-homeostatic-margin proposes that reproductive-stage transitions — especially perimenopause and menopause — shift the immune homeostatic margin and thereby modify the probability of failed post-infectious recovery. The graph records this as `well_supported` but `CONTESTED` (26 support signals, 3 disputes, 53 source references), with the highest risk score in the project.

The crude female PAIS excess is robustly replicated across triggers: post-acute persistence is female-biased in COVID (OR 1.22), dengue fatigue (OR 1.65–1.69), and qualitatively in Q-fever (interpretation:0002-t013-cross-trigger-sex-effect-sizes; proposition:0008-female-excess-concentrates-in-post-acute-persistence). However, decomposition radically reframes the signal. The excess concentrates in self-report or ascertainment-mediated domains and reverses to male-biased in hard vascular and thrombotic endpoints — a reversal that survives coarse severity restriction (proposition:0012-vascular-hard-endpoint-male-reversal-survives-severity-adjustment; interpretation:0005-t042-vascular-male-reversal-survives-severity-adjustment). Objective cognition testing is sex-null while subjective cognitive complaint is female-biased (proposition:0010-cognitive-female-excess-is-self-report-only-absent-in-objective-testing). The dysautonomia female skew is attributable to the pre-existing ~5:1 female POTS baseline, not PAIS amplification (proposition:0009-dysautonomia-female-skew-is-baseline-carried-not-pais-amplified).

The core contested proposition (proposition:0001-reproductive-stage-transition-shifts-the-failed-recovery-threshold, support=3, dispute=3) lacks a formal test: Shah2025 (via interpretation:0003-t018-subphenotype-sex-reproductive-stage) finds the 40–54 female peak unchanged between menopausal and non-menopausal women. No admissible direct HRT→PAIS causal estimate exists (interpretation:0008-t019-hrt-evidence-audit-no-admissible-pais-test). The one objective female-biased signal — persistent inflammatory activation — is substantially testosterone-conditioned and reverse-causation-ambiguous (proposition:0013-immune-domain-partial-hormone-mediated-objective-exception; interpretation:0006-t041-objective-female-biased-subphenotype-search). The confirmatory longitudinal test (task:t028, UKB menopause→PAIS total-effect) is data-gated and unrun.

## Arc

Investigation opened with a plausible female-predominance framing: several PAIS triggers show 2–4:1 female excess in persistence, and the midlife age concentration in long COVID is consistent with a perimenopausal threshold. task:t013 (interpretation:0002-t013-cross-trigger-sex-effect-sizes) confirmed the persistence-concentration pattern across COVID, dengue, and Q-fever, establishing that the female excess emerges in the post-acute phase rather than being inherited from acute female susceptibility — the foundational move for proposition:0008-female-excess-concentrates-in-post-acute-persistence. It also showed the somatic-vs-neuropsychiatric domain dissociation was directionally inconsistent across triggers and unreliable.

task:t018 (interpretation:0003-t018-subphenotype-sex-reproductive-stage) then ran a six-domain decomposition that found a measurement-channel axis as the organizing structure: self-report domains are female-biased, objectively-measured or hard-endpoint domains are sex-null or reversed. The critical tempering result was Shah2025's within-age-band menopause null — the midlife female peak unchanged between menopausal and non-menopausal women — disputing a menopausal-status-specific reading of proposition:0001-reproductive-stage-transition-shifts-the-failed-recovery-threshold while leaving an age/immunosenescence threshold open. interpretation:0003-t018-subphenotype-sex-reproductive-stage deliberately held the vascular male reversal pending severity adjustment.

Two moves then closed that open question and ran the objective discriminator. task:t042 (interpretation:0005-t042-vascular-male-reversal-survives-severity-adjustment) established that the male-biased hard vascular endpoint direction is present both in ambulatory patients (Xie2022 aHR 1.69) and within hospitalized patients (Kopp2024 HR 1.68), minting proposition:0012-vascular-hard-endpoint-male-reversal-survives-severity-adjustment as a genuine domain reversal. task:t041 (interpretation:0006-t041-objective-female-biased-subphenotype-search) probed four objective domains for a female-biased signal; three (autoantibody, autonomic/exercise, small-fiber/gap) reinforced the ascertainment reading, but the immune/inflammatory domain was female-amplified in within-sex case-control designs (Aid2025, Shahbaz2025) while substantially testosterone-conditioned (Silva2024) — establishing the bounded proposition:0013-immune-domain-partial-hormone-mediated-objective-exception and falsifying the strong universal "every objective domain is sex-null or male-biased."

task:t019 (interpretation:0008-t019-hrt-evidence-audit-no-admissible-pais-test) closed the exogenous-hormone audit: no corpus source supplies an admissible HRT→post-acute-PAIS effect estimate, leaving endogenous gonadal-steroid work (Silva2024, Shahbaz2025) as the only live biological channel for the hypothesis.

Current epistemic position: a real but largely measurement-channeled female excess, one bounded hormone-linked immune exception, and a contested core proposition whose decisive test (task:t028) remains blocked on UKB data provisioning.
