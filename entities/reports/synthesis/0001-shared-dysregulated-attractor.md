## State

`hypothesis:0001-shared-dysregulated-attractor` posits that long COVID, ME/CFS, PTLDS, post-dengue fatigue, and related syndromes converge on a single, stable dysregulated attractor in immune-autonomic-metabolic space, reachable from many infectious triggers. The graph records `supported` status (support\_count = 4, dispute\_count = 0, source\_count = 8), but that reflects the proposition bundle's posture, not an empirically confirmed molecular claim; `has_empirical_data` is unset.

Two investigative threads constrain the hypothesis without refuting it. First, `interpretation:0001-cross-trigger-pathway-overlap-reanalysis-t035-null-nonarbitrating` returned a non-arbitrating null: NES rank-concordance between PI-CFS and Q-fever fatigue cohorts was anti-concordant (ρ = −0.563, p\_perm = 0.949) across all six sensitivity cells, but the test was underpowered (2 cohorts, n = 7–10/group, cross-platform) and is recorded as a marginal downward nudge on demonstrability only. Second, `proposition:0011-objective-pem-correlates-are-trigger-and-endpoint-specific-not-one-shared-failure-mode` (support = 4, dispute = 0), grounded in `interpretation:0004-t025-pem-stratified-molecular-gap-and-cross-trigger-cpet-dissociation`, establishes that PEM's objective correlates are trigger- and endpoint-specific: the ME/CFS whole-body CPET decrement does not transfer to long COVID at the same endpoint, even as long-COVID PEM carries a peripheral-muscle OXPHOS lesion. The organizing conjecture survives; a strong "single shared bioenergetic lesion" reading does not.

Open decisive tests: `question:0001-shared-molecular-signature-across-triggers` (harmonized ≥3-trigger multi-omics with full-recovery controls) and `question:0008-formalize-vicious-cycle-attractor-model` (dynamical-systems formalization; bistability/hysteresis against longitudinal data).

## Arc

The hypothesis launched around the convergence-of-triggers idea: symptom overlap across PAIS is underlain by genuine shared physiology, not a coincidental repertoire of organ-system failures. Initial support derived from narrative synthesis — a multi-domain ME/CFS–long COVID parallel, mutually reinforcing vicious-cycle loops, cross-trigger symptom counts — leaving the attractor language qualitative.

The first empirical probe, `interpretation:0001-cross-trigger-pathway-overlap-reanalysis-t035-null-nonarbitrating`, ran in June 2026 against the only available two-trigger public pairing (PI-CFS, GSE14577; Q-fever fatigue, GSE130353). The pre-registered locked resolution order halted at step 2: uniform anti-concordance across six pair × database cells. The pre-committed asymmetric reading held the hypothesis at `proposed` and sharpened the case that only a purpose-built ≥3-trigger harmonized design can adjudicate.

Concurrently, `interpretation:0004-t025-pem-stratified-molecular-gap-and-cross-trigger-cpet-dissociation` identified that the within-cohort PEM-stratified molecular comparison does not exist in any accessible PASC cohort, and that the three-arm cross-trigger CPET pattern encodes a physiological dissociation now formalized as `proposition:0011`. The subsequent amendment, `interpretation:0007-t044-stop-pasc-pem-proteome-severity-unadjusted-gap-stands`, attempted and blocked route (a) toward a severity-adjusted PEM proteome contrast (STOP-PASC/Maestri2025 models univariate, individual-level data gated), adding only a weak blood-proteome anchor (IL1RL1/IL1R2) to `proposition:0011`.

Current epistemic position: cross-trigger convergence is supported at the symptom and review level, not yet at the shared-mechanism level. The deflationary rival (`question:0017-deflationary-alternatives-vs-shared-pathophysiology`) remains genuinely competitive.
