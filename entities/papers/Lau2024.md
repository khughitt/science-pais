# A synbiotic preparation (SIM01) for post-acute COVID-19 syndrome in Hong Kong (RECOVERY)

## Key Contribution

Single-center randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial testing whether a synbiotic preparation
that modulates gut microbiota improves post-acute COVID-19 syndrome symptoms. It is the strongest
interventional hint that the gut axis is not merely an observational biomarker, but the result is still
too broad to prove a specific microbiome mechanism.

## Methods

- **Design:** randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
- **Participants:** 463 adults with PACS at a Hong Kong hospital; randomized 1:1 to SIM01 or vitamin C
  placebo.
- **Intervention:** SIM01 twice daily for 6 months. SIM01 combines anaerobic *Bifidobacterium* strains and
  soluble fibers/prebiotics.
- **Measurements:** symptom questionnaire, quality of life, physical activity, fecal microbiome, and blood
  cytokines.

## Key Findings

- At 6 months, SIM01 improved several symptoms versus placebo, including fatigue, memory loss, difficulty
  concentrating, gastrointestinal upset, and general unwellness.
- The trial reported microbiome shifts in the intended direction: increased beneficial taxa and reduced
  pathogenic taxa.
- The benefit did not clearly translate into quality-of-life or exercise-capacity improvement.

## Relevance

This trial weakly supports `proposition:0031` because perturbing the gut ecosystem improved some PACS
symptoms under randomized conditions. It is especially relevant to `topic:therapeutics-and-clinical-trials`
as a microbiome-targeted intervention with positive symptom endpoints.

## Limitations

- Single-center Hong Kong trial; diet, variant, and microbiome baseline may limit generalizability.
- Composite synbiotic product; mechanism cannot be assigned to one organism, SCFA production, immune
  effects, placebo-response modification, or nonspecific nutritional effects.
- Symptom endpoints improved, but objective functional endpoints did not clearly move.
- Does not establish whether microbiome abnormalities are upstream of established long COVID.
