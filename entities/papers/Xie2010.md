# Analysis of a viral infection model with delayed immune response

## Key Contribution

Compact delayed immune-response viral model analyzing how retarded immune activation changes the
stability of infection equilibria. In the t011 set, this is a clean methods paper for threshold and
stability machinery, but it is biologically generic.

## Methods

- **Model type:** delayed within-host viral infection model.
- **Core variables:** virus/target-cell infection dynamics with delayed immune response.
- **Analysis:** equilibrium stability and Hopf bifurcation.
- **Source status:** triaged from DOI/Crossref metadata and indexed abstract; full text was not locally
  available during t011.

## Key Findings

- The model separates infection-free and infected equilibria and studies their stability.
- Delayed immune response can alter stability and produce oscillatory behavior.
- The paper extends the mathematical toolkit used by Wang2007 into a shorter stability-analysis form.

## Relevance

Relevant to `question:0008` as reusable dynamical-systems machinery. It does not supply a PAIS-ready
state vector or empirical parameterization.

## Limitations

- No post-acute syndrome data, no cross-trigger comparison, and no recovery-vs-chronification cohort.
- Models active viral infection rather than established PAIS after the initiating infection.
- Does not include the multi-node loop architecture h0001 requires.
