# Abnormalities of AMPK activation and glucose uptake in cultured skeletal muscle cells from individuals with chronic fatigue syndrome

- **Authors:** Audrey E. Brown, David E. Jones, Mark Walker, Julia L. Newton
- **Year:** 2015
- **Journal:** PLOS ONE, 10(4), e0122982
- **DOI:** 10.1371/journal.pone.0122982
- **PMID:** 25836975
- **PMCID:** PMC4383615
- **BibTeX key:** Brown2015
- **Source:** PubMed record

## Key Contribution

This study uses cultured skeletal-muscle cells from CFS patients and controls to model contraction-associated exercise stress by electrical pulse stimulation. It is not an in-vivo PEM provocation study, but it provides a tissue-specific mechanistic complement to 31P-MRS: CFS-derived muscle cells fail to activate AMPK and glucose uptake after stimulation despite preserved insulin-responsive glucose uptake.

## Methods

- **Cohort:** skeletal-muscle cultures from 10 CFS subjects and 7 age-matched controls.
- **Model:** in-vitro electrical pulse stimulation for up to 24 hours to mimic contraction-associated exercise.
- **Readouts:** AMPK phosphorylation, glucose uptake, myogenin expression, IL-6 secretion, and related metabolic responses.

## Key Findings

- At baseline, CFS cultures showed increased myogenin expression and reduced IL-6 secretion during differentiation.
- After 16 hours of electrical pulse stimulation, control cultures increased AMPK phosphorylation and glucose uptake.
- CFS cultures did not show the stimulation-induced AMPK phosphorylation or glucose-uptake increase.
- Insulin-stimulated glucose uptake remained intact, localizing the defect to the exercise/contraction response rather than all glucose transport.
- IL-6 secretion in response to stimulation was reduced in CFS cultures.

## Relevance

For `proposition:0030`, this supports a persistent skeletal-muscle-cell abnormality in the response to contraction-like stress. The retention of differences in cultured cells argues against a pure deconditioning or effort explanation, although it does not establish in-vivo PEM timing.

## Limitations

- In-vitro contraction model, not whole-person exercise provocation and not a before/after PEM biopsy.
- Small sample.
- Does not measure OXPHOS capacity directly in intact muscle tissue.
- Because cells are cultured outside their vascular/immune environment, the result cannot by itself decide whether h0006's ischemic upstream step is primary.
