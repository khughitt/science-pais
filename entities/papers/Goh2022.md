## Key Contribution

Goh2022 is a two-patient long-COVID tissue case report using multiplex immunohistochemistry and RNAscope
to detect SARS-CoV-2 nucleocapsid protein and viral RNA in tissues collected **163 and 426 days** after
symptom onset. The viral nucleocapsid signal was observed in appendix, skin, and breast tissue and
co-localized with the macrophage marker **CD68**. Both patients had negative nasopharyngeal PCR testing at
the time of tissue sampling.

## Methods

- **Design:** case report, n=2 long-COVID patients undergoing clinically indicated tissue procedures.
- **Samples:** appendix and skin from patient 1; breast tissue from patient 2.
- **Assays:** multiplex immunohistochemistry for SARS-CoV-2 nucleocapsid protein plus immune/epithelial
  markers including CD68; RNAscope in situ hybridization for SARS-CoV-2 spike RNA.
- **Controls:** normal colon/breast tissue controls shown in assay figures; no matched long-COVID-negative
  post-COVID tissue cohort.

## Key Findings

- Residual SARS-CoV-2 nucleocapsid protein was detected in all three sampled tissues.
- Nucleocapsid protein co-localized with CD68-positive cells, suggesting tissue immune-cell/macrophage
  involvement.
- SARS-CoV-2 RNA was also detected by RNAscope.
- The authors framed the gastrointestinal tract as a plausible SARS-CoV-2 reservoir and called for
  larger controlled studies.

## Relevance

This is the closest available human long-COVID tissue result for the h0002 cross-pathogen
tissue/macrophage-reservoir conjecture (`proposition:0023`). It is **weak support only**: it extends the
tissue/macrophage piece beyond Borrelia into SARS-CoV-2, but it is a two-case report, does not establish
degradation-resistant fragment chemistry, and does not measure an overlapping host proteomic/metabolic
signature comparable to McClune2025.

## Limitations

The n=2 case-report design and clinical-surgery sampling frame make prevalence and specificity
uninterpretable. No prospective symptom association, burden-response, macrophage functional assay, or
host-signature overlap was tested. It should not promote h0002 by itself.
