# Evidence Line: Ryan2022 longitudinal: blunted type-I antiviral-effector ISGs (MX1/OAS3/OASL) in long-COVID referrals at the 6-month bifurcation — reconciled as the dissociated type-I-effector arm, supports (not disputes) the dissociated-IFN reading

## What this line shows

Ryan2022 (*BMC Medicine*) longitudinally profiled 69 convalescents (whole-blood RNA-seq at 12/16/24 wpi)
vs 14 healthy controls, in a re-infection-controlled South Australian cohort. **All** convalescents were
broadly perturbed (>950 DEGs incl. upregulated innate/cytokine signalling) to ~3–4 months; LC-clinic-
referral-specific signatures emerged **only at 24 wpi** (~6 months), among which **type-I antiviral-
effector ISGs MX1, OAS3, OASL were significantly decreased** in referrals vs non-referred convalescents.
Reconciled (`interpretation:0012`), this **blunted type-I-effector arm** is the dissociated counterpart to
the persistent type-II/inflammatory IFN tone — the signature of *tolerized/exhausted innate sensing* under
chronic stimulation. It therefore **supports** `proposition:0025`'s dissociated-IFN clause rather than
disputing the persistent-activation finding of Aid2025.

## Why it is independent

`independent` under `independence_group: ryan2022-calhn-convalescent-longitudinal`. Distinct cohort
(Adelaide, mild-dominant, pre-vaccine, re-infection-controlled), compartment (**whole blood**, not PBMC),
contrast (**referral-vs-non-referral within convalescents**, not LC-vs-recovered/healthy), and readout
(specific terminal ISG transcripts, not GSEA pathway enrichment) from the Aid2025 line
(`evidence-line:0061`). The two are genuinely independent designs converging on a dissociated signature.

## Caveats / scope

`direct_test`, **weak** — its support for the *dissociation* reading is **inferential**, not a within-
study demonstration: (1) the ISG decrease is a **relative** contrast (referrals vs other convalescents),
so it indexes a *differential* blunting, not an absolute below-healthy suppression; (2) only **three**
terminal ISGs (MX1/OAS3/OASL) define it; (3) the LC case definition is **clinic referral**, not a
standardized score; (4) the alternative readings the authors themselves flag — ongoing antigen suppressing
innate sensing, a *pre-existing* IFN-I deficiency predisposing to LC, or exhaustion after chronic low-level
stimulation — are not separated here (only (c) supports the exhaustion-loop reading; an IFN-stimulation
assay on convalescent PBMCs would adjudicate); (5) whole blood is platelet/granulocyte-rich, and the
strongest LC-referral signal in this study is actually **platelet** downregulation. Coded **weak**
accordingly — it corroborates the dissociated-arm interpretation without independently establishing it.
