# Evidence Line: Berende2016 (PLEASE) — null PTLDS antibiotic-retreatment RCT

## What this line shows

The PLEASE trial (N=280 mITT) is the largest placebo-controlled RCT of extended antibiotic therapy for
established PTLDS. After a shared 2-week IV ceftriaxone induction, randomization to 12 additional weeks
of oral doxycycline or clarithromycin/hydroxychloroquine yielded **no benefit over placebo** on the SF-36
Physical-Component Summary score at week 14 (P=0.69; doxycycline vs. placebo +0.2, 95% CI −2.4 to 2.8;
clarithromycin/HCQ vs. placebo +0.9, 95% CI −1.6 to 3.3). All arms improved from baseline (P<0.001), but
the improvement was shared across placebo and active arms — attributable to the ceftriaxone induction,
regression to the mean, or natural trajectory, not to the randomized drug. This **weakly disputes**
`proposition:0020` (antigen clearance rescues established-disease symptoms) from a spirochaetal trigger,
providing the **Borrelia-arm parallel** to the SARS-CoV-2 antiviral nulls (`evidence-lines:0053–0055`).

## Why it is independent

`independence: independent` — distinct pathogen class (spirochaetal *Borrelia* vs SARS-CoV-2), distinct
clearance modality (oral antibiotics vs Mpro inhibitor vs neutralizing mAb), distinct cohort (Netherlands,
PTLDS patients). Mechanistically independent from `evidence-lines:0053–0055`.

## Caveats / scope

**Weak**, for the same structural reason that makes the LC antiviral nulls uninterpretable:

1. **No antigen target-engagement demonstrated.** No residual *Borrelia* antigen, peptidoglycan, or
   fragment was measured at baseline, during, or after treatment. Whether extended antibiotics cleared any
   pathogen-derived molecular signal is unknown. The trial tests "more antibiotic → better outcomes," not
   "clearing antigen → better outcomes."

2. **Shared ceftriaxone induction confounds the comparison.** This is not an antibiotic-vs-none design;
   the control arm received 2 weeks of IV ceftriaxone before placebo. Any pathogen or antigen clearance
   achievable by antibiotic therapy may have already occurred during the induction. The trial tests
   incremental oral dosing, not the full effect of antibiotic exposure.

3. **No antigen-positive enrichment.** Enrollment used clinical diagnosis and serology (IgG/IgM
   immunoblot), not confirmed presence of residual antigen. An unknown fraction of participants may not
   have carried the antigen the retreatment was intended to clear — the same limitation as STOP-PASC
   (`evidence-line:0053`).

Per `discussion:0003` and `interpretation:0011`, these nulls **do not refute antigen persistence**
(`hypothesis:0002`) — a drug that did not demonstrably clear antigen cannot test whether clearing antigen
helps. Consistent with the `proposition:0021` fixed-risk-factor-at-onset reconciliation: if antigen acts
as a determinant at illness onset and becomes non-operative once the self-sustaining chronic state is
established (`hypothesis:0001`), late retreatment would be ineffective without disconfirming the
persistence mechanism.
