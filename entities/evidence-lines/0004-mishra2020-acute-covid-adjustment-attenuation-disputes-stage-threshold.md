# Evidence Line: Mishra2020/Costeira2021 acute-COVID adjustment attenuation disputes a simple stage effect

## What this line shows

`paper:Mishra2020` (and, concordantly, Costeira2021) show that in **acute** COVID, menopausal-status and hormone-proxy associations may **not remain independent predictors** after adjustment for age, severity, comorbidity, route, and indication. This disputes a simple, adjustment-robust reproductive-stage effect of the kind `proposition:0001` asserts.

## Why it is independent

Acute-COVID cohorts distinct from the post-acute hormone cohorts; `independence_group: acute-covid-hormone`.

## Caveats / scope

`model_criticism`, weak — and **scope-limited**: these are *acute*-phase outcomes, not the post-acute persistence `proposition:0001` is about, so the attenuation bounds rather than refutes the post-acute claim. Its more direct bearing is as *support* for the confounding-decomposition claim `proposition:0004` (see `evidence-line:0011`).
