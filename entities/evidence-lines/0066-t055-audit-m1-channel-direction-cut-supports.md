# Evidence Line: t055 audit — PAIS self-report/mixed-origin group differences attenuate or decompose under objective re-measurement (4/4 cut), supports M1

## What this line shows

This line encodes the **M1 cut of the `task:t055` within-corpus audit**
(`interpretation:0015-t055-measurement-channel-audit-of-pais-group-differences`): of the audited group-
difference claims **established in a self-report or mixed-ascertainment channel, 4 of 4 attenuated or
decomposed** under objective, trigger-matched re-measurement, with **no survivors**. The four instances:
`proposition:0010` (cognition — the cleanest, *same-cohort* case: Walitt2024 reports raised subjective
cognitive complaints across five domains with **no** difference on 15 objective neuropsychological tests),
`proposition:0009` (dysautonomia female skew is baseline-carried, not PAIS-amplified), `proposition:0008`
(the crude post-acute female excess decomposes under subphenotyping), and `proposition:0001`
(reproductive-stage threshold attenuates to null within age band). It directly tests and supports
M1 (`proposition:0027`).

## Why it is independent

`independent` — the line is **anchored to Walitt2024**, whose adjudicated same-cohort objective-vs-
subjective cognition contrast is the strongest single instance and is independent of the other arms'
cohorts. The remaining three instances come from distinct cohorts/designs (POTS-baseline literature,
cross-trigger sex meta-analysis, Shah2025), so the 4/4 cut is not a single-cohort artifact. It is **not**
independent of the audit itself: this is one aggregating analysis, which is why a single such line (not
four) is recorded and why M1 should remain fragile until a *prospective* same-cohort re-measurement
(h0008 criterion #2) is run.

## Caveats / scope

`direct_test`, **moderate** — bounded by: (1) **retrospective and project-internal** — only the cognition
instance is a within-cohort objective re-measurement; the other three infer attenuation by comparing
*different* cohorts, itself an M2-type ascertainment confound; (2) the line aggregates a **single audit**,
so it is one vote, not four; (3) it governs only the self-report/mixed-origin subpopulation — the mirror
case `proposition:0012` (objective vascular male reversal) confirms M1's *direction-reversal* limb but, as
a robust objective effect, belongs to the bounded-exception register B and is **not** part of this 4/4
cut.
