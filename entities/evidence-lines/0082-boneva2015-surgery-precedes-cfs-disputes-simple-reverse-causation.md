# Evidence Line: Boneva2015 surgery-before-CFS timing weakly disputes simple reverse causation

## What this line shows

The reverse proposition `proposition:0003` remains live for cross-sectional hormone and reproductive-stage findings: established PAIS can plausibly perturb the HPG axis. Boneva2015, however, adds a limited temporal constraint. In the subset with both gynecologic-surgery dates and CFS-onset dates, hysterectomy/oophorectomy occurred before CFS onset in 71%.

That pattern weakly disputes the strongest simple reverse-causation reading for the surgical-menopause component: "CFS caused the gynecologic surgery / surgical menopause timing."

## Why it is grouped with the supporting line

This is the same Boneva2015 source as `evidence-line:0081`, so it uses `independence: shared-source` and the same `independence_group`. The two evidence lines partition one study's asymmetric message rather than creating two independent cohorts.

## Caveats / scope

`model_criticism`, weak. This does not refute `proposition:0003` generally. The timing check is for surgery, not natural menopause; CFS onset is not necessarily infection onset; and shared gynecologic disease, surgery indications, or healthcare-seeking may precede both surgery and CFS diagnosis. The reverse axis-perturbation claim still requires longitudinal hormone/stage data to adjudicate.
