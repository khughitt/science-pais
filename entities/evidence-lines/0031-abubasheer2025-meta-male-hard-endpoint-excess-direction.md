# Evidence Line: Abubasheer2025 — pooled male excess across hard cardiovascular endpoints

## What this line shows

Abubasheer2025 is a systematic review and meta-analysis of sex-based differences in cardiovascular outcomes in COVID-19. It pools a **male excess across hard endpoints**: VTE **RR 1.43 (95% CI 1.19–1.71)**, myocardial infarction RR 1.24, plus higher ischemic stroke, mortality, and major bleeding (RR 1.22). This establishes that the male *direction* of the vascular reversal is **reproducible across many cohorts**, not an artefact of any single dataset — supporting sub-claim (A) of `proposition:0012` (the domain is male-biased).

## Why it is independent

Aggregates studies methodologically distinct from the two project-cited cohorts; its own `independence_group: abubasheer2025-cv-sex-meta`. It contributes breadth/reproducibility, which neither single cohort can.

## Caveats / scope

`proxy_support`, moderate — deliberately **not** a severity-discriminating line. (1) The pooled studies are predominantly **hospitalized** and the meta does **not** stratify or adjust for acute severity, so this line supports the male *direction* (sub-claim A) but **does not** by itself establish that the bias survives severity adjustment (sub-claim B) — that is carried by `evidence-line:0029` (ambulatory) and `evidence-line:0030` (within-hospitalized). (2) **Aggregator non-independence risk**: a literature meta may share primary studies with the project's other cited cohorts; treated as breadth evidence for direction, not as independent corroboration of the severity-survival claim. (3) Full text was inaccessible (Wiley paywall); pooled point estimates were read from the abstract/PubMed and heterogeneity statistics were not verifiable.
