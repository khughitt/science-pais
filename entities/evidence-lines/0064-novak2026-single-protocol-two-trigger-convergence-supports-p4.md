# Evidence Line: Novak2026 is the first single-protocol head-to-head SFN/autonomic comparison of long COVID and ME/CFS — supports cross-trigger convergence (P4) but reveals a specificity caveat

## What this line shows

Unlike the prior P4 base — convergence inferred *across separate papers* with different methods
(Oaklander2022 long COVID `evidence-line:0038`, Joseph2021 ME/CFS `evidence-line:0043`, Adler2024 PTLDS
`evidence-line:0044`) — Novak2026 documents convergent SFN **and** near-identical autonomic-failure
profiles in **two PAIS arms under one standardized paired-site protocol at one center**: biopsy SFN
67% (long COVID) vs 53% (ME/CFS); reduced orthostatic CBFv 92% vs 88%; widespread autonomic failure
95% vs 89%. This directly answers `evidence-line:0043`'s standing caveat that "convergence is of the
finding, not of a standardized protocol" — here it **is** a standardized-protocol convergence,
strengthening the *quality* (not the cohort count) of the P4 base.

## Why it is independent

`shared-source` — re-uses the **same Novak2026 cohort** as `evidence-line:0063` (P1), so it adds no new
*independent cohort* to the graph; it is the same patients evaluated for a different target (cross-trigger
convergence rather than the bare lesion). Per the project convention (cf. `evidence-line:0043` vs `0039`),
one cohort = one independent vote even across propositions.

## Caveats / scope

`direct_test`, **moderate** — bounded by two consequential caveats: (1) the **ME/CFS arm used ME-ICC/NAM
criteria WITHOUT documented infectious onset**, so its "post-infectious" status is presumptive — it
weakens the strict *infection-triggered* reading of cross-trigger convergence; (2) **specificity
warning** — the study's **hEDS arm (n=290), a non-infectious heritable dysautonomia, shows comparable or
greater SFN (63.3% biopsy; "more pronounced peripheral neurodegeneration")**, demonstrating the
small-fiber substrate is **not exclusive to post-infectious syndromes**. This supports SFN as a
convergent substrate of dysautonomia *broadly* rather than of PAIS *specifically*, and previews the
`pre-registration:0003` "reverse/diagnostic surprise" branch (the specificity-vs-primary-dysautonomia
contrast may be harder to win than lesion existence). Also: referral bias; QASAT metric (per
`evidence-line:0063`); ME/CFS symptom duration longer (chronicity confound).
