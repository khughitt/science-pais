# Evidence Line: Stein2025 immunoadsorption improves autonomic symptoms in β2-AR-AB-elevated post-COVID ME/CFS — proof-of-concept support for immunomodulation modifying trajectory

## What this line shows

Stein2025 is a prospective **immunoadsorption** (IgG depletion) study in β2-adrenergic-receptor-
autoantibody-elevated post-COVID ME/CFS (n=20). **70% responders** on SF-36 Physical Functioning;
COMPASS-31 autonomic symptoms improved (orthostatic / secretomotor / GI sub-domains; **mean −12.23
pts, p=0.001**), **sustained to 6 months**; re-treatment re-improved relapsers. This is
interventional support that lowering antibody burden improves the autonomic trajectory, supporting
`proposition:0019` [@Stein2025].

## Why it is independent

`independent` under its own `independence_group: stein2025-charite-immunoadsorption`. Shares its group
only with the anti-GPCR pathogenicity line (`evidence-line:0048`) drawn from the same cohort.

## Caveats / scope

`direct_test`, **moderate** — bounded by: (1) **uncontrolled** (no sham; large placebo response
expected in post-COVID syndromes — echoes the RituxME open-label → RCT-null cautionary tale); (2)
β2-AR-AB and total IgG fell **equally in responders AND non-responders**, so depletion did **not**
predict response — weakening the causal chain; (3) endpoints are symptomatic / autonomic, **not** a
measured IENFD / structural-lesion trajectory; (4) COI (CellTrend β2-AR-AB diagnostic patent); (5)
severe housebound patients excluded; (6) proof-of-concept only.
