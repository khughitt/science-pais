# Evidence Line: Kharraziha2020 functional α1-AR activity ↔ orthostatic-symptom severity in POTS

## What this line shows

Using a **functional FRET whole-cell β-arrestin-recruitment assay** (not a binding ELISA), Kharraziha2020
found that **α1-adrenergic-receptor (ADRA1) serum activity correlated with orthostatic-symptom burden**
in POTS (Orthostatic Hypotension Questionnaire composite: β = 0.77 OHQ points per SD of activity,
p = 0.009), and the association **survived adjustment for ΔHR and ΔSBP** (p = 0.031). ADRB2, CHRM2, and
OPRL1 activity did **not** correlate with the composite. This is the single best evidence in the corpus
that *functional* (activity-based, not merely binding) GPCR-autoantibody signal tracks autonomic-symptom
severity — the correlational arm of `proposition:0018` and of h0007 promotion criterion #2. The
4-receptor panel separated POTS from controls (AUC 0.88, n = 48 POTS / 25 controls; OHQ subset n = 33) [@Kharraziha2020].

## Why it is independent

`independence: independent` — a distinct cohort (Lund tertiary POTS referral, functional FRET platform),
sharing neither data nor assay platform with the binding-ELISA lines (`0050` Loebel, `0051` Hall) or the
immunoadsorption cohort (`0047`/`0048` Stein). It is the *functional-assay* counterweight to Hall2022's
binding-ELISA null, which is exactly why the two do not cancel: they measure different things.

## Caveats / scope

**Moderate, not strong**, bounded by: (1) the correlating receptor was **α1-AR, not the β-adrenergic /
muscarinic targets** the q0009 hypothesis centers on — a partial, off-target hit; (2) **POTS, not a PAIS
cohort** — no post-infectious selection, so relevance to PAIS dysautonomia is by analogy; (3)
cross-sectional **correlation, not causation** — activity could be epiphenomenal; (4) the assay reads
serum *activity*, an indirect proxy for a specific pathogenic autoantibody; (5) **no small-fiber readout**
— like all four lines here it speaks to autonomic *function*, not the IENFD/SGNFD lesion
(`proposition:0014`/`0015`) that `0018`'s claim ultimately concerns. Supports the autonomic half of
`0018`; silent on the antibody→*lesion* bridge.
