# Evidence Line: Oaklander2022 paired-site skin biopsy finds SFN the most common objective abnormality in prolonged long COVID — supports a structural lesion

## What this line shows

Oaklander2022 is a prospective evaluation of 17 patients with prolonged long COVID (WHO Delphi
definition; SARS-CoV-2 confirmed; 16/17 mild acute illness), using **paired distal (leg) + proximal
(thigh) skin-biopsy IENFD**. 62.5% (10/16) of distal biopsies were abnormal, and SFN was the **most
common objective neuropathy finding**. This is a **direct structural lesion** documented in an
infection-triggered PAIS, using a paired-site protocol capable of detecting a non-length-dependent
pattern — directly supporting `proposition:0014` [@Oaklander2022].

## Why it is independent

`independent` under its own `independence_group: oaklander2022-inspire-longcovid-sfn`. This is the
long-COVID leg, methodologically and by-cohort distinct from the ME/CFS series (Joseph2021) and the
post-vaccine PASC cohort (Limongelli2026).

## Caveats / scope

`direct_test`, **moderate** — bounded by: (1) small n=17; (2) **no healthy and no primary-dysautonomia
controls**; (3) no per-patient non-length-dependent fraction reported; (4) only standard autoimmune
serology (ANA / ESR / SS-A / SS-B), no functional GPCR autoantibodies; (5) the dysimmune mechanism is
inferred from clinical course / apparent treatment response, not from serology.
