# Evidence Line: ME/CFS muscle-bioenergetics body supports a muscle-endpoint abnormality

## What this line shows

The t056 ME/CFS muscle-bioenergetics pass found a positive but heterogeneous body of muscle-endpoint evidence:

- **Jones2012** is the primary load-bearing line: repeated exercise plus 31P-MRS showed excess intramuscular acidosis after comparable work and nearly four-fold delayed pH recovery in the CFS subgroup that generated normal phosphocreatine depletion.
- **Wong1992** independently found faster exhaustion during 31P-NMR gastrocnemius exercise and lower ATP at exhaustion, interpreted as oxidative-metabolism impairment with accelerated glycolysis.
- **Brown2015** showed that CFS-derived skeletal-muscle cells fail to mount the normal AMPK phosphorylation and glucose-uptake response to electrical pulse stimulation, while insulin-responsive glucose uptake remains intact.
- **Bizjak2024** adds a direct muscle-biopsy comparison of CFS and post-COVID syndrome, showing mitochondrial functional/morphological abnormalities but not identical trigger phenotypes.

Together these support `proposition:0030`: ME/CFS has objective skeletal-muscle bioenergetic abnormalities under exercise/contraction-like stress. This is a real update against the old "no ME/CFS muscle-endpoint datum" premise.

## Why it is independent

`independence: independent` relative to the Appelman2024 long-COVID muscle-biopsy line and the Keller2014 ME/CFS whole-body 2-day-CPET line. Internally, the four sources are treated as one consolidated body because they are heterogeneous assays all bearing on the same interim proposition; coding them as four separate lines would overstate precision and independence for h0006-A1.

## Caveats / scope

`strength: moderate` for the local proposition only. This body does **not** demonstrate that ME/CFS and long COVID share the same post-PEM muscle-OXPHOS lesion. The endpoints differ from Appelman2024: pH/phosphocreatine/ATP kinetics, in-vitro AMPK/glucose uptake, and resting biopsy respirometry/morphology are not interchangeable with a day-after-PEM OXPHOS/SDH/fiber-type/myopathy biopsy time-course. The result partially supports h0006's muscle-localization intuition, but it leaves h0006's cross-trigger promotion criterion unmet.
