# Evidence Line: EatonFitch2024 same-panel ME/CFS and long-COVID immune-exhaustion transcriptomics

## What this line shows

EatonFitch2024 profiled PBMC RNA from ME/CFS (n=14), long COVID (n=15), and healthy controls (n=18) with
the NanoString nCounter Immune Exhaustion panel. The study found overlapping pathway themes in ME/CFS and
long COVID, including chemokine signaling, type I and II IFN, IL signaling, CTLA4 signaling, NF-kB
signaling, complement, Treg differences, and exhausted-CD8 signals. ME/CFS showed downregulated IFN and
immunoglobulin genes; long COVID showed dysregulated antigen presentation, cytokine signaling, and immune
activation genes.

This supports the local q0006 proposition that non-COVID PAIS partially recapitulate the LC
IFN/cytokine/exhaustion axis.

## Why it is independent

`independent` under `independence_group: eatonfitch2024-nanostring-mecfs-longcovid-pbmc`. It is a
different cohort, country, assay, and disease comparison from Aid2025 and Ryan2022. It is also a
same-panel LC/ME/CFS comparison rather than a cross-paper inference.

## Caveats / scope

`direct_test`, **weak**. This is not a direct support line for `proposition:0025`'s full LC-specific state
and not evidence for `proposition:0026`'s causal-driver claim. The cohorts are small, the panel is
exhaustion-focused rather than unbiased, and the shared signal is broad pathway-family overlap rather than
the full pattern of sustained IL-6/JAK-STAT/type-II-IFN tone plus blunted type-I antiviral-effector ISGs.
It is therefore coded to a local q0006 generalizability proposition, not to h0003's core bundle.
