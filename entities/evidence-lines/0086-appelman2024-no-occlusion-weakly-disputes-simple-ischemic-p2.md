# Evidence Line: Appelman2024 weakly disputes a simple ischemic-upstream P2

## What this line shows

Appelman2024 strongly supports a provoked muscle lesion, but it complicates a simple ischemic or
microclot-occlusion mechanism. Mitochondrial OXPHOS was impaired in hyperoxic ex-vivo respirometry,
which removes acute oxygen-delivery limitation during the assay. Amyloid-containing deposits were
extravascular and did not occlude capillaries; capillary-to-fiber ratios and intracellular lactate did not
show the simple hypoxia/obstruction pattern.

This weakly disputes the strongest version of h0006 P2: "capillary obstruction or local ischemia directly
causes the PEM muscle lesion."

## Why it is shared-source

The same Appelman2024 source is part of the support for P1 (`evidence-line:0083`). Here it is read as a
mechanism criticism, so it uses `independence: shared-source` and the same localization independence group
rather than counting as a fully independent counterweight.

## Caveats / scope

The line does not rule out functional vascular dysfunction, dysautonomic shunting, endothelial signaling,
or a stage-dependent vascular lesion. It disputes the simple structural occlusion/hypoxia variant.
