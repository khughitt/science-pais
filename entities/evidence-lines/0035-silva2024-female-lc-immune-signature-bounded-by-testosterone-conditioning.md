# Evidence Line: Silva2024 — female LC immune signature, bounded by testosterone conditioning

## What this line shows

Silva2024 (MY-LC, n=165: 53 control-F, 23 control-M, 58 LC-F, 31 LC-M) supplies **both halves** of
`proposition:0013`. It supports the female *direction*: a distinct female LC immune signature
(exhausted and cytokine-secreting CD4/CD8 T cells; elevated EBV/CMV/HSV-2 antibody reactivity;
Female-LCIS AUC 0.88 in held-out testing) is identifiable by sex-symmetric assays. But it also
supplies the **bound** that keeps the proposition a *partial, hormone-linked* exception rather than
a clean categorical-sex positive: **testosterone is the single top predictor** of the sex-based immune
phenotype, lower-testosterone females look immunologically like LC-males (and vice versa), and in a
model with sex × testosterone interaction, **after accounting for testosterone, sex designation is no
longer a significant predictor** of symptom burden or organ-system involvement. The operative
variable appears more closely tied to gonadal-steroid level than categorical sex, while causal
mediation remains unproven [@Silva2024].

## Why it is independent

The MY-LC cohort under its own `independence_group: silva2024-my-lc` (note: methodologically the same
parent cohort family as Klein2023, but Klein2023 is **not** an evidence-line on this proposition, so
no shared-group double-count arises here). Distinct from the Aid2025 (`evidence-line:0033`) and
Shahbaz2025 (`evidence-line:0034`) cohorts.

## Caveats / scope

`proxy_support`, **moderate** — this line is deliberately **double-edged**: it raises confidence that
an objective female-biased immune signal exists *and* lowers confidence that it is a *categorical-sex*
amplification (vs a testosterone/HPG-axis effect that is only partly sex-linked). It ties the
exception to the project's existing gonadal-steroid story (`hypothesis:0005`; the testosterone work in
`paper:Silva2024`, t032, t036). Reverse causation is explicitly unresolved (cross-sectional; LC may
suppress the HPG axis); the paper establishes association, not pre-infection total-effect direction.
The reduced-cytotoxicity (NK/CD8) female pattern it also reports is largely **non-significant** and is
not relied on here.
