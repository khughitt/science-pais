# Evidence Line: Shahbaz2025 testosterone-cytokine inverse correlation supports the pathway-modification mechanism

## What this line shows

`paper:Shahbaz2025` reports testosterone **inversely correlated with pro-inflammatory cytokines** (IL-6, TNF-α, IFN-γ, MCP-1, IL-17a, IP-10) in female long-COVID patients — an association compatible with the hormone–immune link `proposition:0002` posits, observed in patients rather than inferred from general biology.

## Why it is independent

Same Alberta LC cohort as `evidence-line:0003`, but bearing on the **mechanism** proposition rather than the threshold effect; shares `independence_group: alberta-lc` so the two lines do not double-count as independent cohorts.

## Caveats / scope

`proxy_support`, weak: a cross-sectional correlation, direction unproven (low testosterone could be downstream of the inflammation, not upstream); reverse-causation caveat shared with `proposition:0003`.
