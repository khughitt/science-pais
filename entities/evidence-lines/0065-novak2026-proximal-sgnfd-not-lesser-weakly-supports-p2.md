# Evidence Line: Novak2026 group-level proximal SGNFD involvement is not lesser than distal — weak, inferred (not per-subject classified) support for the non-length-dependent pattern (P2)

## What this line shows

Novak2026's paired thigh + calf biopsies show that proximal small-fiber involvement is **not lesser
than distal**, most visibly for **sweat-gland nerve fiber density (SGNFD)**: proximal-thigh SGNFD
abnormality is comparable to distal, and group ENFD-abnormality rates are substantial at the proximal
site. A pattern in which proximal autonomic-fiber loss tracks (or is not exceeded by) distal loss is
inconsistent with a pure length-dependent dying-back gradient and is consistent with a
non-length-dependent / ganglionopathy distribution — weak support for `proposition:0015`.

## Why it is independent

`shared-source` — same Novak2026 cohort as `evidence-line:0063`/`0064`; not a new independent cohort.

## Caveats / scope

`proxy_support`, **weak** — the decisive limitation is identical to Oaklander2022's P2 line
(`evidence-line:0042`): the paper reports **group-level site densities, not a per-subject
non-length-dependent vs length-dependent classification, and no NLD fraction**. The pattern is therefore
**inferred from group means, not measured per patient** — it cannot move P2 from "asserted" to
"measured." Additional bounds: QASAT grading rather than site-specific ≤5th-percentile z-scores (the
metric `proposition:0015`'s measurement model requires); referral-cohort selection. P2 remains the
thinnest leg of h0007 pending the paired-site, per-subject-classified vehicle of `pre-registration:0003`.
