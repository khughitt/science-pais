# Evidence Line: ME/CFS muscle-endpoint data disputes the clean PEM endpoint dichotomy

## What this line shows

`proposition:0011` was originally framed from a three-arm contrast: ME/CFS has a whole-body two-day-CPET PEM signature (Keller2014), long COVID lacks that whole-body decrement in a small PEM-enriched cohort (Gattoni2025), and long COVID has a muscle-level post-PEM OXPHOS lesion (Appelman2024). The t056 body shows that the **absence of a ME/CFS muscle endpoint was overstated**. ME/CFS has in-vivo exercise 31P-MRS muscle abnormalities (Jones2012; Wong1992) and contraction-stimulated skeletal-muscle-cell abnormalities (Brown2015).

This weakly disputes only the clean dichotomy "ME/CFS = whole-body endpoint; long COVID = muscle endpoint." The better reading is: both triggers have objective bioenergetic abnormalities, and ME/CFS already has muscle-endpoint evidence, but the **specific endpoint families remain non-harmonized**. That is more nuanced than the old proposition wording.

## Why it is independent

Independent sources relative to the three lines that originally supported `proposition:0011` (`Keller2014`, `Gattoni2025`, `Appelman2024`). Consolidated under the same `mecfs-muscle-bioenergetics` independence group as `evidence-line:0075` because it is the same t056 evidence body read as a scope criticism.

## Caveats / scope

`dispute_scope: generalization`, weak. This is not a refutation of `proposition:0011`: there is still no same-protocol study showing that ME/CFS and long COVID share the Appelman2024 post-PEM OXPHOS/SDH biopsy phenotype, and the long-COVID whole-body 2-day-CPET null remains a real endpoint-specific tension. The line narrows the claim from "PEM's objective correlate is trigger-specific in kind" to "cross-trigger equivalence depends on harmonizing the objective endpoint, because heterogeneous muscle endpoints can make convergence appear or disappear."
