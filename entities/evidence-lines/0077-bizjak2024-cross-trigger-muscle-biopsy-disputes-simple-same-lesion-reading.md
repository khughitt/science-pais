# Evidence Line: Bizjak2024 disputes a simple same-lesion reading

## What this line shows

Bizjak2024 is the most direct t056 cross-trigger muscle-biopsy comparator: CFS and post-COVID syndrome both show skeletal-muscle mitochondrial abnormalities, but the phenotypes are not identical. Post-COVID syndrome has a complex-I/OXPHOS emphasis, while CFS has more progressed mitochondrial morphological/cristae abnormalities.

That weakly disputes two over-simple readings of `proposition:0011` at once. It disputes the old one-sided dichotomy because CFS has a muscle phenotype too, but it also disputes a naive convergence claim because the CFS and post-COVID muscle phenotypes differ. The honest reading is endpoint-contingent: muscle is a shared measurement domain, not yet a demonstrated shared lesion.

## Why it is independent

Independent from the 31P-MRS/cell-culture ME/CFS exercise/contraction body in `evidence-line:0076` and from the original `Keller2014` / `Gattoni2025` / `Appelman2024` support lines. It is coded separately because it is a direct cross-trigger muscle-biopsy comparison rather than another ME/CFS-only exercise endpoint.

## Caveats / scope

`dispute_scope: generalization`, weak. Bizjak2024 is resting biopsy, not an Appelman-style pre/post-PEM biopsy time course. It does not refute endpoint-specific divergence; it says the discriminating question has moved from "is there any ME/CFS muscle endpoint?" to "which harmonized muscle endpoint, after which provocation, shows convergence or divergence?"
