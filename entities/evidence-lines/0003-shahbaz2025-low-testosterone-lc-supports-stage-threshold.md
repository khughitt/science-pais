# Evidence Line: Shahbaz2025 (Alberta) low non-dominant sex hormone in LC weakly supports the stage threshold

## What this line shows

In a University of Alberta LC/ME-CFS cohort (n=140, CCC criteria, ~12 mo post-infection), `paper:Shahbaz2025` finds **reduced testosterone in female LC and reduced estradiol in male LC**. A consistent low-non-dominant-sex-hormone pattern across an independent cohort reinforces the gonadal-steroid → recovery gradient `proposition:0001` predicts.

## Why it is independent

A separate country, cohort, and author network from MY-LC (`evidence-line:0002`) and from the UKB SHBG literature; `independence_group: alberta-lc`.

## Caveats / scope

`proxy_support`, weak: **cross-sectional / case-control**, so it is reverse-causation-ambiguous and equally supports `proposition:0003` (see `evidence-line:0009`). ME/CFS-defined subset; gonadal steroids, not reproductive stage; no pre-infection baseline.
