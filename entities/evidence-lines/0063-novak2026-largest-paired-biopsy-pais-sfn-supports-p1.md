# Evidence Line: Novak2026 is the largest paired-site biopsy series documenting SFN in both long COVID and ME/CFS vs 0% in controls — supports a structural lesion

## What this line shows

Novak2026 (PLoS One; referral-center retrospective) applied **paired proximal thigh + distal calf**
skin biopsies (ENFD + SGNFD, PGP9.5/Therapath) to the largest PAIS biopsy series in the corpus:
**long COVID n=143, ME/CFS n=170**, healthy controls n=73. Biopsy-confirmed SFN was **67.2% (long
COVID)** and **52.6% (ME/CFS)** versus **0% in controls** (91.4% / 82.9% when combined with
electrochemical skin conductance). A real, objectively measured peripheral small-fiber lesion is present
in a substantial subset of both PAIS arms — directly supporting `proposition:0014` [@Novak2026].

## Why it is independent

`independent` under its own `independence_group: novak2026-referral-cohort-paired-biopsy` — a cohort and
center methodologically distinct from Oaklander2022 (`evidence-line:0038`), Joseph2021
(`evidence-line:0039`), and Limongelli2026. It adds a genuinely new independent cohort to the P1 base.

## Caveats / scope

`direct_test`, **moderate** (not strong) — bounded by: (1) **QASAT age/sex-adjusted grading (abnormal =
score > 0), more inclusive than the conventional ≤5th-percentile IENFD cutoff** — so these prevalence
figures likely overstate SFN relative to percentile-cutoff studies and are not directly comparable to
them (a `topic:measurement-ascertainment-artifacts-in-pais` concern); (2) referral-center selection bias
(enriches severity); (3) retrospective single-center design; (4) historical controls without parallel
laboratory data. The direction (substantial lesion-positive subset vs 0% controls) is robust; the
*magnitude* is metric-sensitive.
