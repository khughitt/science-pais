# Evidence Line: Shahbaz2025 cross-sectional hormone deficit is equally consistent with infection-driven axis perturbation

## What this line shows

The reduced testosterone (female LC) and estradiol (male LC) in `paper:Shahbaz2025` are, being **case-control / cross-sectional**, equally consistent with infection/PAIS suppressing the reproductive axis — support for `proposition:0003`, mirroring the same datum's weak support for the forward effect (`evidence-line:0003`).

## Why it is independent

Same Alberta cohort as `evidence-line:0003`; shares `independence_group: alberta-lc`. The paper itself flags reverse causation as unresolved (menopausal status not collected).

## Caveats / scope

`proxy_support`, weak: non-discriminating between directions by design. Together with `evidence-line:0008`, it establishes that the two best non-UKB hormone cohorts are **symmetric** between `proposition:0001` and `proposition:0003`.
