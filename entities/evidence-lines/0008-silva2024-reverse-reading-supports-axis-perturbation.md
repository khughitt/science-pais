# Evidence Line: Silva2024 cross-sectional hormone deficit is equally consistent with infection-driven axis perturbation

## What this line shows

The lower testosterone in female long-COVID reported by `paper:Silva2024` is, because it is **cross-sectional**, equally readable as **LC-driven HPG-axis suppression** — i.e. support for `proposition:0003` (reverse direction) rather than for the forward threshold effect. This line makes that symmetric reading explicit.

## Why it is independent

Same MY-LC datum as `evidence-line:0002`; shares `independence_group: my-lc`. It is deliberately the *same observation* targeted at the opposite-direction proposition — the design's "one statement, many contexts" applied to a reverse-causal rival.

## Caveats / scope

`proxy_support`, weak: by construction it cannot discriminate forward from reverse; it raises P← to parity with P→ on this datum, not above it. The discriminating evidence is temporal (pre-infection vs within-person post-infection hormone measurement).
