# Evidence Line: Stein2025 anti-GPCR (β2-AR) autoantibody selection + depletion-associated autonomic improvement — weak support for anti-GPCR pathogenicity

## What this line shows

In Stein2025, anti-GPCR (β2-adrenergic-receptor) autoantibody elevation was the prospective
**enrollment criterion**, and its depletion **co-occurred with autonomic (COMPASS-31) improvement** —
indirect support that anti-GPCR autoantibodies are pathogenically involved in the autonomic phenotype,
supporting `proposition:0018`.

## Why it is independent

**Not independent.** `independence: shared-source` — this line shares `independence_group:
stein2025-charite-immunoadsorption` and the same cohort with `evidence-line:0047`. It re-reads the
Stein2025 immunoadsorption series for the anti-GPCR-pathogenicity target and cannot be counted as a
separate cohort of support [@Stein2025].

## Caveats / scope

`indirect`, **weak** — bounded by: (1) non-independence from `evidence-line:0047` (same cohort); (2)
β2-AR-AB reduction did **not** predict who responded (equal depletion in responders / non-responders),
so the anti-GPCR → symptom causal link is **not established**; (3) uncontrolled; (4) COI.
Counter-context: deSa2026's pathogenic autoantibodies were **non-GPCR** (MED20 / USP5) and did **not**
recapitulate the autonomic axis, so anti-GPCR-specific autonomic pathogenicity remains essentially
unproven.
