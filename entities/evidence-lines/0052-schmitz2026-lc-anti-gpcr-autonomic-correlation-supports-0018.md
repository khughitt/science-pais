# Evidence Line: Schmitz2026 long-COVID anti-GPCR ↔ HRV/BP, with a null in-vitro functional test

## What this line shows

Schmitz2026 supplies the **long-COVID trigger arm**: in post-COVID patients selected for HRV alteration,
anti-GPCR autoantibodies (AT1R, AT2R, β1/β2-AR, M1/M3-AChR, and a novel **CXCR3** target) **correlated
with HRV alterations and ambulatory/stress blood pressure** — extending the titer↔autonomic-function
association of `proposition:0018` into long COVID specifically. CXCR3 antibodies correlated with higher
24h mean arterial pressure; M1-AChR + CXCR3 with higher stress-test BP.

## Why it is independent

`independence: independent` — a separate German long-COVID cohort; no data overlap with the POTS
(`0049`/`0051`) or ME/CFS (`0050`) lines. Adds trigger breadth (LC) to the autonomic-correlation signal.

## Caveats / scope

**Weak**, and partly self-limiting: (1) **purely associational/cross-sectional** — no passive transfer,
no depletion; (2) its own **in-vitro functional test was NULL** — patient antibodies produced no direct
beat-frequency/amplitude effect on hiPSC-derived cardiomyocytes, i.e. the paper itself failed to
demonstrate functional pathogenicity, *disputing* the agonist-antibody mechanism even as the correlation
supports involvement; (3) the assay is **binding-inferred (CellTrend), not confirmed functional**
(`[UNVERIFIED]` in `paper:Schmitz2026`), exposing it to the Hall2022 specificity critique; (4) cohort n,
case definition, time-since-infection, and per-receptor statistics are all `[UNVERIFIED]` (full text not
accessible); (5) the paper **explicitly does not connect anti-GPCR antibodies to the small-fiber lesion**
(no IENFD/skin biopsy/QSART) — the antibody→*lesion* bridge of `0018` stays untested. Counts as a thin
brick for the autonomic half only, with an embedded functional null.
