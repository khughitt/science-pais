# Evidence Line: Scheibenbogen2024 weakly supports h0006 P3

## What this line shows

Scheibenbogen2024 summarizes the Wirth/Scheibenbogen acquired ischemic mitochondrial myopathy model:
hypoperfusion under exertion drives proton excess, Na/H exchange raises intracellular sodium, Na/K-ATPase
clearance becomes insufficient, and Na/Ca exchange reverses, causing calcium overload and subsarcolemmal
mitochondrial injury. The review cites a 23Na-MRI anchor for elevated intracellular skeletal-muscle sodium
in ME/CFS and links sodium to handgrip weakness.

This weakly supports h0006 P3, the ionic mediator leg.

## Caveats / scope

The support is weak because the complete mediator chain has not been measured end-to-end in provoked PAIS
muscle. The source is a narrative mechanistic synthesis by the model's authors, not an independent
confirmatory experiment, and direct human muscle calcium-overload data are missing in the current corpus.
