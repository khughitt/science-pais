# Evidence Line: M3/M2 scope-criticism of the standardized cross-trigger SFN convergence claim

## What this line shows

This is the **h0008-M3 (`proposition:0029`) scope-criticism edge** that `proposition:0017`'s belief
surface was previously missing. `proposition:0017` is graded **well_supported** off three lines —
Adler2024 (weak, narrative review), Joseph2021 (ME/CFS, distal-only), Novak2026 (the only single-protocol
study) — but those establish that **SFN recurs as a *finding* across heterogeneous protocols**, not that
**the same standardized substrate recurs across distinct post-infectious triggers**, which is the claim as
titled and the claim M3 contests. Read critically, the strongest datum *against* the strong reading comes
from the same paper that most supports it:

- **Only 2 of the 3 claimed triggers are tested under one protocol.** Novak2026's paired thigh+calf design
  spans long COVID and ME/CFS only; **PTLDS** (the *Borrelia* arm) is carried entirely by Adler2024, a
  narrative review with no standardized biopsy. So "recurs across PTLDS, long COVID, and ME/CFS *with a
  comparable signature*" is, at the standardized-protocol level, a **2-trigger** observation.
- **The substrate is not specific to post-infectious etiology.** In the same protocol, **hEDS (n=290), a
  non-infectious heritable dysautonomia, shows SFN comparable to or greater than the PAIS arms** ("more
  pronounced peripheral neurodegeneration"). A "shared end-organ failure mode *reachable from many
  infections*" that appears just as strongly in a heritable non-infectious condition is not established as
  *infection-linked* convergence — the finding is consistent with a final-common-pathway lesion reached by
  many routes, infectious or not.

Net: this disputes the **standardized / infection-specific shared-substrate** reading of `proposition:0017`
while leaving the weak reading (SFN is found in each syndrome) intact. It is M3's claim made concrete on a
**second phenotype** (SFN, alongside PEM): whether cross-trigger *sameness* is real depends on running one
protocol across the triggers — and the one time that was partly done, it came back 2-trigger and
non-specific.

## Why it is independent

**Not independent.** `independence: shared-source`, `independence_group:
novak2026-referral-cohort-paired-biopsy` — it is a *critical re-reading of the same Novak2026 cohort* that
supplies supporting lines `0064`/`0065`, not a new cohort. That is the honest coding: the scope-criticism
and the convergence-support are two readings of one dataset, so the model should not count them as
independent. The PTLDS-gap and hEDS-non-specificity points are facts internal to that single standardized
study plus the structural absence of a 3-trigger protocol.

## Caveats / scope

`dispute_scope: generalization`, **weak, deliberately not a refutation.** This line does **not** dispute
`proposition:0014` (the lesion exists) or the weak reading of `proposition:0017` (SFN is documented in each
syndrome). It disputes only the **standardized-substrate / infection-specific** strength of the convergence
claim — exactly the strength M3 (`proposition:0029`) says is endpoint/protocol-contingent and M2
(`proposition:0028`) says is ascertainment-sensitive. The decisive test is the data-gated
`pre-registration:0003` (standardized paired-site biopsy across triggers **with a clean
primary-dysautonomia control arm**), which is simultaneously h0007's promotion criterion #1 and the place
M3's SFN instance would be confirmed or dissolved. Until it runs, the honest state is: convergence of the
*finding* is well-supported; convergence of a *standardized infection-specific substrate* is untested.
