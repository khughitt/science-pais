# Evidence Line: Averyanova2022 route/timing-dependent hormone biology weakly supports HRT context-dependence

## What this line shows

`paper:Averyanova2022`'s account of hormone action on immune/endothelial/hemostatic pathways implies route-, dose-, and timing-dependent effects — the biological basis for `proposition:0006`'s claim that any HRT effect on PAIS would be heterogeneous rather than uniform.

## Why it is independent

`independence_group: averyanova-mech` (same review as `evidence-line:0006`, retargeted to the HRT-heterogeneity proposition).

## Caveats / scope

`proxy_support`, weak and **analogical**: general hormone biology, not an HRT–PAIS outcome study. This proposition is currently under-evidenced for a *direct* test — flagged for `task:t019` (HRT-evidence audit); the absence of a direct PAIS-outcome HRT line is itself a fragility signal.
