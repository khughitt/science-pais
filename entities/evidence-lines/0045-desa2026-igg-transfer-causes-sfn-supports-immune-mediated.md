# Evidence Line: deSa2026 long-COVID IgG passive transfer reduces intraepidermal nerve fibers in mice — causal support for immune-mediated SFN

## What this line shows

deSa2026 is the **causal anchor**: total IgG purified from long-COVID patients, passive-transferred
intraperitoneally to mice, **reduces intraepidermal nerve-fiber volume/count (SFN)** vs healthy-donor
IgG, in a **donor-symptom-matched pattern** (85–90% concordance for pain/dysautonomia donors). It
elevates autoantibody → SFN from association to **in-vivo causation**, directly supporting
`proposition:0016`. This is the one **experimental** identification in the batch [@deSa2026].

## Why it is independent

`independent` under its own `independence_group: desa2026-mylc-igg-transfer`. It is the sole
experimental passive-transfer line, distinct from all observational SFN and autoantibody lines.

## Caveats / scope

`direct_test`, **strong** — bounded by: (1) long COVID only (**single trigger**); (2) **bulk IgG**,
not antigen-specific; (3) the validated autoantigens (MED20, USP5) are **non-GPCR**; (4) the
cardiovascular-autonomic axis (BP/HR) was **not recapitulated**; (5) short observation window; (6)
female mice; (7) only ~5% BBB penetrance. Strong for immune-mediation of **somatic SFN**, but does
**not** establish anti-GPCR or autonomic-axis causation.
