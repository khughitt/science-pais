# Evidence Line: Cheetham2023 — objective deficit is symptom-status-linked, sex a non-significant covariate

## What this line shows

Cheetham2023 is the strongest controlled-longitudinal design for **objective** cognition in long COVID (online battery, n≈3,335, with controls). The objective accuracy deficit scales with **ongoing-symptom status** (largest for ≥12-week non-recovered, β≈−0.22 SD vs asymptomatic controls), is **absent in the fully recovered**, and persists to ~2 years; **sex enters only as a non-significant adjustment covariate**. This constrains `proposition:0010` by establishing that the objective deficit is real and explained by symptom-status rather than sex — the backdrop against which the female excess appears only in the subjective channel.

## Why it is independent

The COVID Symptom Study Biobank cohort (UK), `independence_group: cheetham-css-cog`, distinct from the two Spanish/other cross-sectional cognition lines.

## Caveats / scope

`background_constraint`, **weak** for the sex-specific claim: Cheetham2023 **did not perform a sex-stratified objective analysis**, so the absence of a reported sex×deficit interaction is a **gap, not a positive null** — it cannot directly establish an objective sex-null, only that sex was not a significant adjusted predictor and that the deficit tracks symptom status. It is weighted as a constraint on interpretation, not a direct sex-contrast line.
