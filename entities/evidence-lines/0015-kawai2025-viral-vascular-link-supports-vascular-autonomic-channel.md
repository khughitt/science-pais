# Evidence Line: Kawai2025 viral-vascular/thromboinflammatory link supports the vascular-autonomic channel

## What this line shows

`paper:Kawai2025` links viral infection to vascular and thromboinflammatory outcomes that could interact with menopause-associated cardiometabolic and endothelial risk — analogical support for `proposition:0007`'s non-adaptive-autoimmune vascular/autonomic channel.

## Why it is independent

`independence_group: kawai-vascular`; a vascular-biology source distinct from the hormone and clinical-overlap lines.

## Caveats / scope

`proxy_support`, weak and analogical: it establishes a plausible viral→vascular interaction with menopausal risk, not that this channel actually carries part of the reproductive-stage→PAIS effect (a mediation claim, which is derived/n-ary and not authored here).
