# Evidence Line: Keller2014 ME/CFS whole-body 2-day-CPET decrement (positive reference arm)

## What this line shows

Keller2014 establishes the **positive reference** for `proposition:0011`: in Fukuda-defined ME/CFS, a second maximal CPET 24 h after the first yields a **13.8% fall in VO₂peak**, with confirmed maximal effort on both days (RER ≥ 1.1, unchanged between tests) and against a healthy/deconditioned test–retest reproducibility of ≤7% (r ≥ 0.95). Fifty percent of participants were reclassified to a more impaired functional category on day 2. This is the well-validated **whole-body** objective signature of PEM in ME/CFS, and — because deconditioning predicts a stable, not a worsened, day-2 result — it excludes deconditioning as the explanation [@Keller2014].

## Why it is independent

Distinct cohort, trigger (ME/CFS, not post-COVID), and design from the long-COVID lines; its own `independence_group: keller2014-mecfs-2day-cpet`. It supplies the arm of the cross-trigger contrast against which the long-COVID whole-body null (`evidence-line:0027`) and the long-COVID muscle-level positive (`evidence-line:0028`) are read.

## Caveats / scope

`background_constraint`, moderate: this is the *reference* arm, not itself a cross-trigger observation — it shows the ME/CFS whole-body signature exists and is robust, but says nothing on its own about long COVID. Fukuda case definition (broader than CCC/ICC); the 2-day-CPET decrement, while replicated across several ME/CFS cohorts, is the established whole-body marker this proposition treats as the comparator, so it carries the proposition only in combination with the long-COVID lines.
