# Evidence Line: Muscle-localization body supports h0006 P1

## What this line shows

The corpus now contains a positive, multi-endpoint muscle-localization body for PEM-relevant
bioenergetic pathology:

- **Appelman2024** directly demonstrates a long-COVID post-PEM skeletal-muscle lesion by paired
  pre/post-exertion biopsy: reduced OXPHOS capacity, a patient-specific post-exercise SDH decline,
  glycolytic fiber shift, immune infiltration, and exercise-exacerbated myopathic injury.
- **Joseph2023** synthesizes invasive CPET physiology in ME/CFS and PASC showing impaired peripheral O2
  extraction and preload failure, localizing exertional limitation to the exercising periphery rather
  than simple deconditioning or primary cardiac limitation.
- **t056 / `proposition:0030`** shows that ME/CFS also has objective exercise/contraction muscle
  bioenergetic abnormalities (Jones2012, Wong1992, Brown2015, Bizjak2024), retiring the old "no ME/CFS
  muscle-endpoint datum" premise.

Together these support h0006 P1: skeletal muscle is a material site of the PEM-relevant bioenergetic
deficit.

## Why it is consolidated

The line consolidates heterogeneous endpoints because they all bear on the same **localization** claim.
It does not assert that the endpoints are equivalent. The cross-trigger/same-lesion question remains
separate and is tracked by `discussion:0004`, `proposition:0029`, and h0006's promotion criterion #3.

## Caveats / scope

`strength: moderate` for P1 only. This line does not support the full ischemic-ionic cascade. Appelman2024
cannot determine whether the mitochondrial defect is primary or secondary; Joseph2023 is a narrative
review of iCPET evidence; the ME/CFS t056 body is not Appelman-equivalent.
