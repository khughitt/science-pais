# Evidence Line: Walitt2024 weakly disputes a primary muscle-substrate reading

## What this line shows

Walitt2024 is the deepest rigorously adjudicated PI-ME/CFS phenotype in the corpus and frames the motor
abnormality around central effort-preference/autonomic circuitry rather than a classical peripheral
myopathy. It reports no group difference in basal PBMC mitochondrial OCR/ECAR, resting energy expenditure,
muscle fiber type, or standard small-fiber density, and interprets reduced VO2 peak through deconditioning
and chronotropic/autonomic features.

This weakly disputes the strongest reading of h0006 P1: that skeletal muscle is the primary PEM substrate
across PI-ME/CFS.

## Caveats / scope

This is not a refutation of the provoked muscle-localization claim. Walitt2024 did not run an
Appelman-style pre/post-PEM muscle biopsy or 31P-MRS muscle recovery protocol, and its CPET subset was
small. The line is therefore `dispute_scope: generalization`: it constrains extrapolation from
long-COVID muscle biopsy and ME/CFS 31P-MRS/cell-culture endpoints to a universal primary muscle lesion.
