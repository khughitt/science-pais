# Evidence Line: Scheibenbogen2024 weakly supports h0006 P4

## What this line shows

Scheibenbogen2024 proposes that once mitochondrial injury and ionic dysregulation are established, reduced
ATP and increased ROS worsen ion-pump function and vascular regulation, feeding back into further
perfusion and mitochondrial injury. Appelman2024's observation that exercise worsens long-COVID muscle
OXPHOS/SDH/myopathic features is compatible with exertion aggravating an existing local lesion rather than
producing normal recovery.

This weakly supports h0006 P4, the self-perpetuating-lesion leg.

## Why it is shared-source

This line comes from the same AIMM model as `evidence-line:0087`; it is coded `shared-source` so the two
mechanistic legs do not masquerade as independent confirmations.

## Caveats / scope

This is not a longitudinal proof of positive feedback. The corpus has provoked worsening and a model of
self-maintenance, but not serial evidence that the muscle lesion persists through the proposed feedback
loop or exits when perfusion/ion handling is corrected.
