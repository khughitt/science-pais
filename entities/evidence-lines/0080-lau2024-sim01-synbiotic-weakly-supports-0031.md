# Evidence Line: SIM01 synbiotic perturbation in PACS

## What this line shows

Lau2024 randomized 463 adults with PACS to 6 months of SIM01 or vitamin C placebo. SIM01, a composite
synbiotic containing anaerobic *Bifidobacterium* strains and soluble fibers, improved several symptoms
versus placebo, including fatigue, memory loss, difficulty concentrating, gastrointestinal upset, and
general unwellness. It also shifted the gut microbiome in the intended direction [@Lau2024].

This weakly supports `proposition:0031` because a randomized perturbation of the gut ecosystem moved
some PACS symptoms.

## Why it is independent

`independence: independent` — separate trial design, participants, and intervention from the observational
long-COVID CUHK microbiome line and from the ME/CFS microbiome studies.

## Caveats / scope

**Weak** despite interventional design. SIM01 is a broad synbiotic, so the active mechanism cannot be
assigned to one organism, SCFA production, barrier repair, immune modulation, or nonspecific nutritional
effects. Symptom endpoints improved, but quality-of-life and physical-activity/exercise-capacity signals
were not clearly improved. This line supports modifiability, not proof that microbiome dysbiosis is the
primary PAIS driver.
