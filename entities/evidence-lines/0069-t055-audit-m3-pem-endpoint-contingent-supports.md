# Evidence Line: t055 audit — cross-trigger PEM correlate is endpoint-contingent, weakly supports M3

## What this line shows

The **single determinate M3 instance** carried by the `task:t055` audit (`interpretation:0015`), via
`interpretation:0004-t025-pem-stratified-molecular-gap-and-cross-trigger-cpet-dissociation` and
`proposition:0011`: the ME/CFS whole-body **two-day-CPET** PEM decrement does **not** transfer to long
COVID at that endpoint, *even though* a long-COVID muscle OXPHOS lesion exists (Appelman2024). Whether the
two triggers look like one "shared PEM mechanism" therefore depends on **which objective endpoint is
chosen** (two-day CPET vs muscle bioenergetics) — the defining M3 pattern. Supports M3
(`proposition:0029`).

## Why it is independent

`independent` as a cohort (Appelman2024 long-COVID muscle biopsy) but **isolated**: it is the *only*
determinate M3 instance in the corpus, so M3 rests on a single phenotype (PEM).

## Caveats / scope

`proxy_support`, **weak** — M3 is asserted on **one phenotype** and is therefore a structural pattern, not
yet a regularity; belief should stay speculative until a second independent instance (a different PAIS
phenotype whose cross-trigger sameness flips with endpoint choice) appears. Note the dual character: this
is an *objective-origin* claim that is nonetheless artifact-consistent, which is exactly why the t055
audit rejected a clean "objective ⇒ survives" rule. The most likely site of a second instance is the
standardized-protocol SFN convergence test (`proposition:0017`, currently UNTESTED via
`pre-registration:0003`).
