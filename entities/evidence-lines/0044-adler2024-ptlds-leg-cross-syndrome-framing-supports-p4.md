# Evidence Line: Adler2024 supplies the PTLDS leg and explicit cross-syndrome SFN framing — weak support for P4

## What this line shows

Adler2024 supplies the **PTLDS leg** — a narrative review citing a series in which **10/10**
well-defined PTLDS patients had abnormal IENFD and/or SGNFD on skin biopsy — plus explicit PTLDS ↔
long COVID ↔ ME/CFS cross-syndrome mechanistic framing (SFN proposed as a **final common pathway**).
This gives weak support to `proposition:0017` [@Adler2024].

## Why it is independent

`independent` under its own `independence_group: adler2024-ptlds-review`. It contributes the PTLDS
trigger, distinct from the long-COVID and ME/CFS legs.

## Caveats / scope

`indirect`, **weak** — bounded by: (1) narrative review (**no PRISMA**); (2) small cited series; (3)
no primary-dysautonomia controls; (4) the non-length-dependent pattern is **asserted, not measured**;
(5) weak as a primary evidence source.
